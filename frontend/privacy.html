Trust & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2024

1. Introduction

AI Real Estate Explorer ("we", "our", or "the platform") is committed to protecting your personal privacy and maintaining transparent data handling policies. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect when you explore properties, engage with the AI Advisor, and export analytical reports.

2. Information We Process

Search Criteria & Questionnaire Preferences: We process your indicated property preferences (e.g. target city, budget intervals, bedroom configurations, and desired amenities) solely to generate real-time match rankings and comparative reports.

Session Identifiers: We utilize an anonymous client session identifier (stored locally via your browser's localStorage) to maintain your saved wishlist, comparison trays, and search profile across sessions. We do not track you across third-party domains.

3. Artificial Intelligence & LLM Interactions

When you utilize natural language query extraction or interact with the AI Advisor consultant, your prompt inputs are processed through secure, stateless API pipelines. We do not use your proprietary search criteria to train public foundation models without explicit authorization.

4. Data Storage & Local Persistence

Your comparison lists, saved properties, and questionnaire states are persisted within your local browser storage. You may clear this data at any time through your browser's application settings or by clicking the "Reset Profile" buttons within the platform.

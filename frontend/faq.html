Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our matching engine, market data, and platform mechanics.

Our matching engine evaluates 5 core dimensions: Location Proximity (30%), Budget Proximity (25%), Room & Area Capacity (20%), Specific Amenities (15%), and Investment Yield Alignment (10%). Rather than arbitrary AI guesses, each score is mathematically weighted and transparently presented with full breakdowns.
All property records, prices, appreciation curves, and market statistics currently shown are part of a curated [Demo Dataset]. They reflect realistic market benchmarks for Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Dubai, and London, but should not be taken as live verified legal deeds or active commercial brokerage agreements.
